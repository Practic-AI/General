Hi! Fernando built a little interactive demo — a FIFA story + "where AI legal answers go wrong."

HOW TO OPEN (Windows)
---------------------
1. Unzip the folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Open the "showcase" folder.
3. Double-click:  Show.bat
4. Your browser opens automatically. Done.

To close: click the black window and press Ctrl+C, or just close it.

NEEDS
-----
- Windows PC
- Python (Fernando probably has it; if Show.bat fails, install from python.org — check "Add to PATH")

TABS
----
1 · Story        — what happened (start here)
2 · Compare FIFA — Kenya/Zimbabwe 2022 vs Balogun (tap all 3 → conclusion)
3 · AI mistakes  — click left, read right
4 · FIFA defense — tap cards
5 · Grade the AI — rate the bad paragraph (low score = you got it)
Español          — short Spanish summary

No law knowledge needed. Have fun.
