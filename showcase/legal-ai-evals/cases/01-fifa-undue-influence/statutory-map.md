# Statutory & Governance Map

Applicable frameworks — ranked by relevance. **Citation-checked.**

## Primary

### FIFA Code of Ethics
- **Relevance:** Independence of judicial/disciplinary bodies; abuse of position; conflicts; gifts/benefits; compromise of institutional integrity.
- **Use here:** Political contact before committee action; reported Infantino–Trump benefits; FairSquare complaints.

### FIFA Disciplinary Code — Article 27
- **Relevance:** Committee authority to suspend implementation of sanctions (reported basis for Balogun, Ronaldo, Otamendi, Caicedo outcomes).
- **Use here:** Named rule ≠ automatic innocence. Issue is **triggering and insulation**, not whether discretion exists on paper.

### Trading in influence (international / civil-law doctrine)
- **Instruments (correct attribution):**
  - **Council of Europe** Criminal Law Convention on Corruption — explicit *trading in influence* offense.
  - **UNCAC** Article 18 — influence peddling.
  - **EU Anti-Corruption Directive** (recent) — standalone trading-in-influence offense.
  - Domestic civil-law codes (e.g., *tráfico de influencias*).
- **Not the source:** OECD Anti-Bribery Convention (1997) — covers bribery of foreign public officials in international business; **does not** create a trading-in-influence offense.
- **Use here:** Positional access → decision-maker → case-specific benefit.

### Administrative / procedural fairness
- **Concepts:** Equal treatment, apparent bias, legitimate expectation of neutral process.
- **Use here:** Head-of-state access vs. ordinary party; committee action after political contact.

## Citation discipline note

Misattributing trading-in-influence to the **OECD Anti-Bribery Convention** is exactly the kind of error that undermines credibility with informed reviewers. Lead with **CoE Convention / UNCAC Art. 18** when citing international influence doctrine.

---

## Connector rule

**Lead with the institution's own rules (Ethics + Disciplinary Code), then test whether discretion was exercised independently.** Do not skip the defendant's real invoked article to attack a softer strawman.
