One number from a file we actually counted

The United States has 28 Cirrus per million people.

Uruguay, at that same density, would hold ~95 airplanes. Publicly we can point to about zero still on a CX tail.

US = 9,386 tails we pulled from the FAA file. FL = 869. UY “95” is a density thought-experiment, not a forecast. CX-PPP (SR22) showed up in the 2010s and was later talked about as sold to Argentina.

1

Delaware has more Cirrus paper than Uruguay has people-in-airplanes we can find.

552 N-numbers in a state of ~1 million. That’s titling, not flying. The insight for UY: count sociedades and Argentine paper, not just CX paint. A Montevideo owner can keep the airplane on LV- or N- and still live here.

2

Minnesota’s “553” is a factory, not a market.

363 of those are still on the dealer certificate. Same trap in LATAM: don’t read a partner’s demo fleet as demand. San Fernando will always look busier than Punta.

3

75% of the US file is SR22 / SR22T.

That’s the SKU. Not a Vision Jet launch in Carrasco. Brazil just certified the G3 jet (6 Aug 2026). Uruguay is a piston, owner-pilot, used-then-new problem.

4

63% of US Cirrus sit in an LLC.

If we sold here like we sold in Florida, we’d be talking to holdings and family offices, not “piloto privado con Cessna 182.” The current UY charter menu is still Cessna / King Air.

The territory is already sold

Cielo S.A. (San Fernando) publicly claims AR + UY + PY + CL. A “UY entry” that ignores them is a partner collision, not a greenfield.

Service desert

~110 nm

Carrasco → San Fernando. About 45 minutes in the airplane you would be selling. There is no public Cirrus Authorized Service Center inside Uruguay. Hide that and the sale dies at the first annual.

When the market actually exists

Punta (SULS) is a season, not a country. Aviasur’s Carrasco FBO just grew 600 → 1,500 m². Rocha is being talked as the next strip. Demo in Dec–Feb or don’t bother.

What the data says to do

  1. Measure CX before pitching 95. DINACIA list vs Cessna / Piper / Diamond. Until then “empty” is a working hypothesis.
  2. Ask Cielo for the UY pipeline. Lost deals, Argentine owners based in Punta, N- / LV- tails that sleep here.
  3. One SULS weekend, not an office. SR22T, CAPS, the triangle SUMU–SULS–SADF.
  4. Sell the ferry. “We pick it up for the annual at SADF.” Makes the 110 nm a product.
